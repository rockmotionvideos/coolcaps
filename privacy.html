Your videos stay yours

Privacy policy · Last updated 14 November 2026

The short version
  • Your videos stay on your phone. Adding captions and exporting happen on your device. Only the sound is sent, securely, to write the caption text.
  • No account, no sign-up, no ads, no tracking.
  • We don't keep your sound, and we don't sell your personal information.

Your videos

Your video file is never uploaded. Drawing the captions, exporting and your Library all happen on your phone.

The sound, for writing captions

To write the captions, Cool Caps sends only the sound of your video (not the picture) over an encrypted connection to a speech-to-text service run by Groq, through a small server we operate that adds our access key. Groq turns the speech into words and sends them back. We don't store the sound or the words on any server of ours, and we don't link them to you. Groq handles the sound under its own privacy terms. If you turn off Cloud transcription in Settings, or your phone is offline, the captions are written by a speech model that runs on your phone and nothing is sent.

What's stored on your phone

To make the app work, Cool Caps saves a few things in your browser's storage on this device only:

  • Videos you export (your Library), until you delete them.
  • Your settings and saved caption styles.
  • Your streak and video count.
  • The speech model, so it doesn't need downloading again.

We can't see any of this. You can delete it at any time: clear your Library in Settings, or delete this site's data in your browser settings.

Things the app downloads

Like any website, these services receive basic technical information such as your IP address and browser type. None of them receive your videos.

  • Hugging Face (huggingface.co): the speech recognition models, downloaded once and kept on your phone.
  • jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net): the speech recognition software.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com): the fonts used in the app and your captions.
  • GitHub Pages (github.io): the host that serves the app itself.

Buying Pro

In the Google Play app, Cool Caps Pro is a subscription handled by Google Play. Google processes your payment under its own privacy policy, and we never see your card details. The app only asks Google Play whether your Google account has an active Pro subscription, so it can turn Pro on.

Sharing styles

When you share a caption style, the link contains only the style's settings and the name you gave it (for example colours, font and words per screen). It doesn't contain your videos or anything about you.

Children

Cool Caps isn't aimed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.
